## 🌌 SECTION 4: MEMORY, TIME, AND CAUSALITY

### Question 11: The Structure of Memory

- **The significance question:** How do you decide what's worth remembering? Everything? Or do you need to evaluate importance?

**Emotional Memory (Valence, intensity, associations):**
